# Crew portraits

Put one image per crew member here, named after the call sign in lower case: `vega.jpg` for VEGA. JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF work. Square images look best; they are cropped to a square.

A crew member with no file here shows the first two letters of the call sign instead.

The sample roster is fictional. Six of its people have portraits cropped from a promotional photo for the film The Martian (20th Century Fox, 2015): `vega`, `okafor`, `haddad`, `lindqvist`, `nakamura` and `orlov`. The photo is copyrighted and shows the film's actors. It is not NASA material and we hold no license for it, so replace these files before the app is published.
